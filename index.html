About Us

Excellence at the Heart of Rwandan Healthcare

Founded to serve the Rwanda Defence Force, Kanombe Military Hospital has grown into one of Rwanda's leading referral hospitals, serving both military personnel and the civilian population with pride.

🏥
National Referral Center
We receive complex referrals from across the country, handling cases that require specialized surgical and medical expertise.
🎓
Training & Research
Affiliated with the University of Rwanda, we train the next generation of Rwandan medical professionals.
🌍
International Standards
Our protocols meet WHO and international best-practice standards, ensuring quality across every department.
